# Overview

"No Way Home" is a post-apocalyptic PvPvE MMO game with turn-based tactics, featuring a dynamic combat system, base building, resource management, and crafting. Players can align with either surviving humans or stranded aliens, exploring the world, improving bases, researching technologies, and expanding squads. The shared world map enhances unpredictability and excitement of player interactions. The game's art style is a blend of hardcore aesthetics and cartoonish visuals, contributing to a cohesive post-apocalyptic atmosphere.

# Storyline

A big alien ship crashes into earth, devastating a large area around the impact site and sending an EMP shockwave around the world which breaks all sorts of things. Earthlings panic, start to shoot and loot and basically pile onto the damage done by the shockwave.

The aliens in the ship were protected during the impact by their advanced technology, but the ship used up all its energy in the process and is now running in battery saver mode, leaving the aliens vulnerable, who are waking up from their stasis slumber in a hostile world, with a dysfunctional ship and *no way home*. Panic ensues, aliens also start to shoot and loot and fall apart into small groups, each fighting for their own survival first.

Panicked earthlings also attacked the crashed ship, and now the aliens are in even more panic than before crashing. You see, the aliens were themselves being attacked by another entity and on the run across the galaxy. The ship has been already damaged during the alien’s escape and while zipping at near light through space, the ship slammed into voyager 1, and the ship’s AI was in high hopes to find a safe harbor on earth. Boy, was it wrong.

Each player will craft their own backstory. In the early game, some plot points will guide character developement and will be used to introduce new concepts step-by-step to the player.

Players will be able to trigger regional and global events on their own or collectively. For example, the establishment of a radio network which enables in-game communication might be triggered by having a sufficient number of bases being fitted with radio towers. Seemingly random environmental events also can be used as  plot points, cutting travel and communication routes.

With each species’ increasing understanding of their new roomies, a number of plot points might involve the rise of new factions and ideologies. 

One ultimate late-stage plot point might be the looming arrival of the entity the alies fled from in the first place.

# Progression

Player progression involves exploring the world, setting up and improving bases, researching technologies, expanding squads and turn-based combat episodes.

Discovery of new areas or technologies will trigger new plot points.

# Core Gameplay Mechanics

## Player Characters

The player chooses if he wants to play humans or aliens and starts off with a squad of two random characters. The prime objective is for the squad to survive and grow.

Early in the game, the player encounters NPCs randomly, some of whom can be persuaded into joining the player’s squad.

Once the player has established a base, the number of applicants will increase. But mouths need to be fed, so the player will need to choose wisely whom to accept, whom to turn down and maybe whom to kick, too.

All characters will share the same stat sheets and “skill trees”, there are no class barriers. This is important to allow for a seamless transition between early game, where a few characters do lots of different tasks to late game where most characters in the squad are specialized. This will also allow for a larger variety in play styles, where a mechanic or a medic may be used in the base as well as in combat (this in turn will allow for a wider variety of combat scenarios/ quests, where one combat situation might require you to have more mechanical skills than firepower).

Customization is focused on weapons, armor and utensils. Customization of clothing might be an monetization strategy.

## Base building

Players have the option to build and improve bases, choosing between nomadic or settled lifestyles. Bases act as hubs for resource management, crafting, and strategic planning.

In XCOM and XCOM:TFTD you were building bases to launch missions from. In XCOM2, your base was mobile. Here these concepts will be combined. Each player decides at the beginning which style he want to start with and he’ll get a random storyline matching his desired style.
A nomad might start with a RockStar tour bus, an APC or a yacht. A settled player might start with a desolate military outpost, an refurbished WWII bunker or the basement of an eccentric prepper.

As players gather more resources, they will improve their bases and might even add other mobile or stationary bases to their army.

## Squad

- [ ]  Explain how the squad system works.
- [ ]  Include details on recruitment, permanent death, and potential traitor mechanics.

Similar to XCOM, the emphasis is on the group, not the individuals, so its expected to loose a figher at some point, but you will also be able to recruit new members on various occasions. You will grow your group, fight other human or alien groups, gather and loot resources, explore the world and maybe even find a way to survive without shooting everybody else.

All the people (and aliens) in your squad are unique (i.e. random). They have random names, random visual features and background stories and random attributes. This is different from XCOM where rookies all start equal.

There will also be no different alien morphologies. All aliens look basically similar, while some are thinner others are bulkier, smaller and bigger etc.

The members capabilities will be tied to classes, but the classes are not so easy to uncover. They will show in the types of weapons used or in special abilities used or equipment carried. It won't be as obvious as with XCOM aliens. 

Death is permanent. If you loose someone, they’re gone forever. If everybody in your squad is dead, it’s game over.

## Research

- [ ]  Detail how research works in the game
- [ ]  Discuss technological advancements and their impact

As your squad grows, you will find team mates which are smarter than others. In addition to crafting more intricate items with a higher success rate, they can conduct research. Later in the game you will find research equipment and laboratories which support your efforts. 

As in XCOM research will be primarily focusing on making use of the other factions technologies. Humans might investigate alien weaponry and energy sources. Aliens might research earths biome (to improve feed efficiency) or human weapons (as a low-energy alternative to their armory). Both races will want to combine the Technologie trees for hybrid weapons (an emp bullet might penetrate an energy shield and deliver the emp blast on impact, a hybrid weapon that renders em shields useless) and other hybrid tech (aliens might find that using human gut microbes improves the efficiency of their food processing machines).

Research will open up new options to craft and new items and resources (and cadavers) found will open up new options for research.

## Realtime

- [ ]  Explain the real-time aspects, including travel and planning.
- [ ]  Address how players can mitigate travel time frustrations.

When you need to travel to an incident, you will actually take the time to travel there. For example, if you decide to take on a job 100km away from your current location and you only have a jeep, it will take you 60-90 minutes to arrive, and only *then* you enter the combat.

This will add a strategic element, insofar as players need to plan ahead when theyll have time.

Need to find ways to compensate for the travel time, so its not too frustrating:

- Configure squad to do auto-reconnaissance: gives you a better insight into the combat map and surroundings as well
- Configure squad to do auto-gathering: will give you insight into combat map surroundings and you might find valuable goods like canned foods, gasoline, ammunition
- Pedal-to-the-metal mode: slash 1/3 of the travel time for double the gasoline
